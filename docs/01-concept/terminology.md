# Terminology

Initial core terms:

- **Entity** — a person, place, object, organization, concept or other identifiable item.
- **Predicate** — a property or relation attributed to one or more entities.
- **Proposition** — a statement represented as having semantic content that can be evaluated or compared.
- **Quantifier** — expressions such as all, some, none or at least one.
- **Modality** — necessity, obligation, permission, possibility and related meanings.
- **Scope** — the part of an expression affected by an operator such as negation or modality.
- **Entailment** — information that follows logically from represented statements and rules.
- **Analysis profile** — specialization of the common engine for a text type.
- **Finding** — a reported observation, difference, ambiguity or error.
