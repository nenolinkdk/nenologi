# Contradictions

Contradiction is a logical relation between represented propositions: under the selected interpretation they cannot both be true. It is separate from a difference, which reports what changed between texts.

Core v0.1 recognizes only the narrow case where explicit polarity is the sole change over an otherwise aligned proposition. It emits one `NEGATION_CHANGE` and sets the comparison relation to `CONTRADICTORY`. General contradiction and natural-language inference remain out of scope.
